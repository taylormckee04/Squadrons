Squadrons — Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

Squadrons is a browser game you can play at squadrons.onrender.com. This page explains, in plain language, what information the game touches and what happens to it. The short version: single player collects nothing at all, and multiplayer sign-in stores nothing.

Playing without signing in

Single player needs no account and no sign-in. The game runs entirely in your browser and sends no personal information anywhere.

Signing in with Google (multiplayer only)

Multiplayer asks you to sign in with Google so other players can see who they are playing with. When you sign in, Google shares three things with Squadrons: your name, your email address, and your profile picture. Squadrons never sees or receives your Google password — sign-in happens entirely on Google's side.

How that information is used

  • To confirm to the game server that you are really signed in (the server checks your sign-in with Google when you connect).
  • To show your name and picture to the other players in your multiplayer lobby and match.

That is all. Squadrons does not use your information for advertising, does not sell it, and does not share it with third parties for marketing.

Storage

Squadrons has no accounts database. Your name, email address, and picture are held only in the game server's memory while you are connected, and are discarded when you disconnect. They are never written to a database or saved to disk.

Cookies

Squadrons itself sets no tracking or advertising cookies. The Google sign-in process may set cookies on Google's own domains; those are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

Squadrons is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email shown on the Squadrons sign-in consent screen.
