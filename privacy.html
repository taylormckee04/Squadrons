Squadrons — Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

Squadrons is a browser game you can play at squadrons.onrender.com. This page explains, in plain language, what information the game touches and what happens to it. The short version: single player collects nothing at all, and multiplayer stores only your account ID and the gamertag you choose.

Playing without signing in

Single player needs no account and no sign-in. The game runs entirely in your browser and sends no personal information anywhere.

Signing in with Google (multiplayer only)

Multiplayer asks you to sign in with Google so the game can tell players apart. When you sign in, Google shares three things with Squadrons: your name, your email address, and your profile picture, along with a Google account ID number that identifies your account. Squadrons never sees or receives your Google password — sign-in happens entirely on Google's side.

How that information is used

  • To confirm to the game server that you are really signed in (the server checks your sign-in with Google when you connect).
  • Your account ID number is the key your gamertag hangs on (see Storage below) — it is how the game knows which gamertag is yours when you come back.

Your Google name, email address, and picture are never shown to other players. Other players see only your gamertag — the display name you choose yourself. Squadrons does not use your information for advertising, does not sell it, and does not share it with third parties for marketing.

Storage

Squadrons keeps a small accounts database (hosted by Neon, a Postgres database provider). For each signed-in player it stores exactly two things:

  • Your Google account ID number (not your email address).
  • The gamertag you chose. Gamertags are unique — the database is also how the game makes sure two players can't hold the same name.

If you change your gamertag, the name you gave up is kept on a locked list for 30 days so nobody else can grab it the moment you drop it. Nothing else is written to the database: your Google name, email address, and picture are held only in the game server's memory while you are connected, are never written to the database or saved to disk, and are discarded when you disconnect. Match results and ratings are not recorded.

Cookies

Squadrons itself sets no tracking or advertising cookies. The Google sign-in process may set cookies on Google's own domains; those are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

Squadrons is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email shown on the Squadrons sign-in consent screen.
